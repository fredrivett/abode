# Reviewing Books

Saved books have a **Reading** section in the side panel, where you can track and review them.

## Tracking and reviewing

1. Open a saved book.
2. Under **Reading**, set a status: **Want to read**, **Reading**, **Read** or **Did not finish**.
3. Once you've started, add the dates you started and finished (to the day, month or just the year), a star rating, and a review.

Your books are also grouped into **Want to read**, **Reading** and **Read** shelves automatically.

## Sharing a review

1. Open the book and click **Share review** (or **Share**, before you've rated or reviewed it) at the top of the **Reading** section.
2. You'll see a preview of how the link looks when you post it in a chat or on social media: the cover, your rating, the start of your review and when you read it.
3. Click **Copy link**, **Copy review** (your review as text, with the book and the link) or, on your phone, **Share…** to use your phone's share menu.

The link opens a page with the book and your full review. Anything you haven't filled in is left out, so you can share a rating without a review, or a review without a rating.

Readers can save the book to their own <Abode /> from that page (when it was saved from a link). People who aren't on <Abode /> yet join with one of your invites; when you have none left, or if you turn this off from the share dialog or in **Settings → Invites**, they join the waitlist instead.

Sharing a review turns on link sharing for the book, so anyone with the link can see it along with your status, dates, rating and review. Your reading progress and notes stay private. To stop sharing, turn off **Share via link** in the book's **Share** settings.

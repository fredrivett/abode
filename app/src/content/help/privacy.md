# Item privacy

Everything you save is private to you unless you share it. Each item's **Privacy** setting decides whether it can also appear where others can see it, and whether it shows openly on your own screen.

Open an item and choose one of three levels under **Privacy**:

- **Default**: the item can appear in your public rooms, and you can share it by link.
- **Keep out of public rooms**: it never shows in a public room, even one whose filters match it. You can still share it by link. Scanned documents and PDFs start at this level, as they're often personal.
- **Sensitive**: only you can see it, and it's kept out of sight on your own screen. Use it for things like bank statements, medical letters or ID documents.

## Sensitive items

A sensitive item is still saved, analysed and searchable like anything else, but:

- **It's blurred everywhere it appears**: on your dashboard, in search results and in your rooms. Its title, text and image stay hidden. It shows as **Sensitive item** in your browser tab, even while it's open.
- **Opening it asks for your password.** Once you've entered it, sensitive items stay open to you for **2 minutes**, and every sensitive item you open restarts that time. After that, you'll be asked again.
- **Nobody else can see it.** It never shows in a public room, and a share link to it stops working while it's sensitive. Change it back and the same link works again.

AI assistants you've connected with an access token can still find and read sensitive items, as can your [data export](/help/exporting). Both are yours alone.

Sensitive keeps things out of sight of someone looking at your screen. It doesn't protect against someone using your account while you're signed in, so lock your computer when you step away.

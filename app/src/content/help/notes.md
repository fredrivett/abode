# Writing Notes

Notes are things you write yourself, saved alongside everything else in <Abode />.

## Writing a note

The first card on your dashboard is a blank note. Click it and start typing.

- The first line is the note's title. Leave it empty for an untitled note.
- Your note is kept as a draft while you write, so you can close the tab and pick it up later.
- Click **Save**, or press ⌘ Enter (Ctrl Enter on Windows), to add it to your items.
- **Clear** empties the note. Click it twice to confirm, so one stray click can't lose your writing.

## Writing in full screen

For anything longer, hover over the note card and click the expand arrow in its bottom right corner. The note opens in a large window, with the same text as the card.

The side panel shows where the note's details, tags and rooms will go. When you click **Save**, the window becomes the saved note, so you can add tags or put it in a room straight away.

Close the window without saving and your text stays on the card as a draft.

## Editing a saved note

Open a saved note to keep writing in it. Changes save as you type. The first line is still the title: edit it there, and the card and search pick up the new title.

The title is plain text, so bold, links and line breaks there are dropped. Use the lines below it for formatting.

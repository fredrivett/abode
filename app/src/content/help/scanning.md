# Scanning Documents

Scan letters, receipts, forms and notes straight into your <Abode /> with your phone's camera, with no app to install. Scans are saved as searchable documents, and can have as many pages as you need (up to 30).

## Scanning a document

1. Tap **+** (Add item), then **Scan a document**, and allow camera access when asked.
2. Point the camera at the page. <Abode /> outlines the page once it finds it.
3. Hold steady. In **Auto** mode the ring around the shutter fills while the page is still, and the page is captured after a few seconds. Or tap the shutter yourself at any time.
4. The page is straightened and cleaned up, then shown for review.

Tap **Auto** in the top corner to switch to **Manual** if you'd rather always capture yourself. It remembers your choice.

Already have a photo of a document? Tap the gallery icon next to the shutter to import it instead. The page is found and cleaned up in the same way.

## Reviewing pages

After each capture you can:

- **Add page** to scan the next page of the same document
- Switch between **B&W** (the "scanned" look, best for text), **Greyscale** (keeps shading, e.g. pencil or photos) and **Colour**. New pages use whichever you picked last
- **Rotate**, **Retake** or **Delete** the page you're looking at
- Swipe between pages, or tap **Arrange** to reorder them

Tap **Save** when you're done, or **Cancel** to discard the scan.

## Finding your documents

Saved documents appear in your <Abode /> like any other item, with a badge showing how many pages they have. Open one and swipe, use the on-screen arrows, or press ← and → to page through it.

Scans are often personal, so documents are kept **out of public rooms** by default: adding one to a public room won't show it there. To show a document in your public rooms, open it and turn off **Exclude from public rooms**.

The text on each page is read automatically once the document has finished processing, so you can find it by searching for words in it. Where its text can be read, the document is also named from it (who it's from, what it is and its date, e.g. "Energy bill, March 2026"), so searching for the sender's name finds it too. Otherwise it's named from how the first page looks. Rename it any time; your title is kept if the document is processed again. To see only your documents, filter with `@type:document`.

## Troubleshooting

- **No camera?** The scanner needs a browser that can use your camera, over a secure (https) connection. On a computer without a camera, import a photo instead.
- **Camera blocked?** Allow camera access for <Abode /> in your browser's site settings, then open the scanner again.
- **Page not detected?** Try a darker background behind the page, better light, or move a little closer so the page fills more of the frame.

# Highlighting Articles

Saved articles open in a clean reader view where you can highlight the parts worth keeping.

## Highlighting

1. Open a saved article.
2. Select the text you want to keep. Choose **Highlight** from the menu that appears.

Your highlights are listed under **Highlights** in the side panel. Click one to jump to it in the article.

To remove a highlight, click it in the article and choose **Delete**, or hover over it in the side panel and click the bin icon.

## Sharing a highlight

1. Click a highlight in the article and choose **Share**, or hover over it in the side panel and click the share icon.
2. You'll see a preview of how the link looks when you post it in a chat or on social media.
3. Click **Copy link**, **Copy quote** (the quote with the article's title and the link) or, on your phone, **Share…** to use your phone's share menu.

The link opens a page with your highlight at the top, then the full article underneath. Readers can click the highlight to see it in context.

People who aren't on <Abode /> yet can save the article to their own <Abode /> from that page, joining with one of your invites. When you have none left, they join the waitlist instead, and you're credited for bringing them. You can turn this off from the share dialog or in **Settings → Invites**.

Sharing a highlight turns on link sharing for the article, so anyone with the link can read it. Your other highlights stay private unless you turn on **Include my highlights** in the article's **Share** settings. To stop sharing, turn off **Share via link** there.
